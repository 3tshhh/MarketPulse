Labeling checklist
  • 1 only if all hold: the move in the orange 5-minute window clearly stands out from the surrounding hours; volume in those minutes is visibly elevated; it isn't a data artifact (a lone wick that snaps back on tiny volume).
  • Typical 0: a slow drift that just crossed the threshold, a move that's ordinary for a very volatile day, a one- or two-tick change on a micro-priced coin.
  • Write a few words in the note for every 0, and for interesting 1s (market-wide drop, listing news).
